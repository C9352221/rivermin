What We Do

The Work of the Ministry

One mission, carried out through preaching, discipleship, healing, the local church, and the training of laborers for the harvest.

✝

Preaching the Gospel

Proclaiming the good news of Jesus Christ to those who have never heard it or never understood it, with a proven track record of reaching souls across the nations.

📖

Discipleship

Our local church is committed to making true disciples. We partner with churches who can follow up with new believers in foreign fields.

🕊

Healing & Deliverance

Praying for the sick and the oppressed to receive healing, freedom, and liberty, demonstrating the love and power of Christ.

Global Evangelism

Reaching the Nations

Since 2022, River Ministries has traveled to preach the Gospel across Guatemala, Brazil, Chiapas, Tijuana, England, Scotland, and Juárez, Mexico. Thousands of men and women have committed themselves to follow Jesus, the sick have been healed, and local partners continue the work of discipleship.

The Local Church

A Growing Church in Juárez

What began as a children's ministry has relaunched as a fully operational, growing local church, the home base for evangelism, discipleship, and the training of the next generation of ministers.

Coming Soon

Bible University

God has called us to raise up a new breed of ministers. A Bible university is in the planning stage, working in dynamic synergy with the local church to equip anointed, bold, uncompromising laborers for the harvest.

Ministry Headquarters

Building for the Harvest

We are presently building our ministry headquarters in Juárez, Mexico. Construction has commenced on phase one of five total phases, laying the foundation for a church and university that will serve the region and the nations for generations to come.
